# Deleting an account

Google Play (Data safety) and App Store Connect both ask for a web page
where somebody can delete their account without installing the app. The
family has one page for every a-Paw app:

    https://spotapaw.github.io/care-a-paw-site/account/

Sign in, reset a password, change an email, delete the account (it calls
`delete_my_account`). Use that URL in this app's Play Console Data safety
form and in App Store Connect. The same page is linked from Your account
inside the app, under Look & feel.

Written 3 Oct 2026, from the Care agent's note on the shared board.
